# 00 Project Scope

## System Identity

- **Project Name:** Online School Fees Monitoring and Payment Information System
- **Institution:** Mother Perpetua Parochial School
- **Baseline Document:** Academic Project Proposal Baseline

## Target Roles & Core Modules

Eventually supports:

1. School Administrators (reporting, settings, audit)
2. Cashiers / Finance Staff (OTC payments, assessments, ledgers)
3. Parents / Guardians (fee visibility, online payment simulation, history)
4. Student Records & Assessments
5. Digital Receipts & Notifications

## Strict Scope Boundaries

- Do **not** invent school policies, fee amounts, or penalty structures.
- Consult the proponents' completed questionnaire before implementing unclear rules.
- Do not claim production deployment readiness at the capstone stage.
- Current branch (`chore/project-foundation`) must contain zero school-fee business feature code.
