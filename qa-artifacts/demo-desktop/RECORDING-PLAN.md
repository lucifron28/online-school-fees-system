# Core workflow desktop recording

Captured with a 1440×900 desktop viewport on 2026-08-27 against `https://online-school-fees.vercel.app`. The browser raster output is 1425×891 because the browser reserves space for its scrollbars and chrome.

## Demo sequence

1. Parent signs in and opens the payment form for Bianca Reyes (`DEMO-0002`).
2. Parent confirms the GCash destination, enters the amount and reference, attaches the fictional proof, and submits it.
3. Finance Staff opens Payment Proofs, checks the student, amount, destination, reference, and proof, then approves the pending proof.
4. Parent refreshes Payment Proofs and opens the system-generated receipt.
5. Student opens the dashboard and payment history to show the student-facing ledger and receipt access.
6. Administrator opens Transactions and Reports to show the posted payment and reconciliation totals.

## Recording notes

- Keep the browser at desktop width 1440×900 and zoom at 100%.
- Use the demo accounts only. The proof and payment shown here are fictional.
- If a core step errors or shows overflow, stop the take, reset and reseed the demo data, fix the defect, and restart from step 1.
- The browser session can display and capture the workflow, but this environment does not provide a video-recorder output. Use the same sequence with a desktop screen recorder for the final MP4.
